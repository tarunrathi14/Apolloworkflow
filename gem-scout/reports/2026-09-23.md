**What this shows**
- **Your regular competitors:** Shakambhari Ispat (THERMOCON), SKA Ispat (Sarthak TMT), SPS Steels (Elegant), Shyam Steel, SAIL, and TATA TISCON with its dealers.
- **Why bidders lose:** resellers offering someone else's brand keep getting disqualified. That happened to 4 RASHMI dealers on one bid, plus Shilpi Trading twice. As the OEM with your own BIS licence, you avoid the most common cause of disqualification. Upload the IS 1786 licence and test certificates cleanly.
- **Price benchmark:** GeM L1 for 8 mm Fe 500D was about ₹70k per MT delivered in April. Recheck against the current market before quoting.
- **Possible partners:** civil contractors who win mixed and works bids (like the Kinnaur BOQ) have to buy TMT. They are sales leads for you, not competitors.
